# Video Walkthrough Script (90–120 Seconds)
### Caprae Capital Full-Stack Challenge — Acquisition Fit Workbench / Sourcing Command Center

---

### [0:00 – 0:15] The Core Sourcing Problem
> *"Hi everyone. Today I'm presenting the **Acquisition Fit Workbench: Deal Sourcing Command Center**.  
> Lead discovery tools like SaaSquatch give searchers and PE teams hundreds of companies. But discovery isn't the real bottleneck. The scarce resource is **analyst research and diligence time**.  
> This workbench solves that: it separates **strategic thesis fit** from **data confidence** to tell you which targets to investigate first, and exactly what to verify next."*

---

### [0:15 – 0:30] Executive Dashboard & Command Center
*(Screen shows Executive View with KPI Insight Strip)*
> *"When opening the application, we enter the **Executive View**—designed for an investment lead or searcher.  
> Right away, the Insight Strip highlights 20 **Priority Targets**, 13 **Verify First** candidates, and an average pipeline fit of 52.  
> Notice our dataset disclosure: 160 realistic companies with deliberate duplicates and missing fields to prove our qualification engine without fake AI claims."*

---

### [0:30 – 0:45] The Opportunity Matrix (Fit × Confidence)
*(Cursor points to the interactive Opportunity Matrix scatter plot)*
> *"Here is our core differentiator: the **Opportunity Matrix**.  
> Traditional tools sort by raw scores. But high fit alone isn't enough—you need to know if you can trust the data.  
> The X-axis measures Data Confidence; the Y-axis measures Thesis Fit.  
> In the top-right, we have **Priority Targets**—high fit and high confidence, ready for outreach.  
> In the top-left, we have **Verify First**—companies that match our criteria but have data gaps like missing emails or unconfirmed revenue."*

---

### [0:45 – 1:00] Target Analysis: "Why This Target?" & Next Best Action
*(Click on a top-right point in the matrix; Lead Detail Drawer slides open)*
> *"Let's click a priority target. The drawer delivers an executive summary in five seconds:  
> **'Why This Target?'** summarizes the firmographic match.  
> The **'Next Best Action'** gives deterministic guidance—in this case, *'Prioritize for initial owner research'*.  
> Below that, a prioritized **Verification Gaps** checklist surfaces any missing data points before our outreach begins."*

---

### [1:00 – 1:15] View Toggle: Executive View → Research View
*(Click the top toggle: EXECUTIVE VIEW → RESEARCH VIEW)*
> *"When it's time for granular pipeline work, we toggle to **Research View**.  
> Here we have our full 11-column data grid with server-side pagination, multi-criteria filters, and keyword search.  
> We can filter by priority, industry, or state, star targets for our persistent **Shortlist**, and export filtered results to CSV."*

---

### [1:15 – 1:30] Side-by-Side Target Comparison
*(Select two or three shortlisted targets using checkboxes; click 'Compare (2/3)')*
> *"Next, I'll select targets to compare and click **Compare Targets**.  
> This opens a side-by-side evaluation matrix across revenue, headcount, operating history, and leadership.  
> Notice the objective, neutral analysis at the top: identifying the strongest fit against our thesis, the highest data confidence, and which target requires the most verification."*

---

### [1:30 – 1:45] One-Click Acquisition Brief
*(Open drawer, click 'Generate Acquisition Brief')*
> *"With one click, we can generate a **Preliminary Acquisition Research Brief**.  
> This formats the snapshot, thesis alignment factors, data gap checklist, and next action into a clean, printable briefing sheet for pre-diligence meetings—with instant PDF export."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:00] Business Value & Engineering Integrity
> *"Under the hood, this is powered by FastAPI, SQLite with indexed queries, React 19, and Tailwind CSS. The entire backend has 76 automated tests passing with zero failures.  
> Every insight is deterministic, testable, and grounded in real data.  
> Instead of wading through hundreds of unranked leads, searchers save tens of hours every week focusing only on high-conviction targets. Thank you."*
