## What is Caprae's mission?
Caprae Capital's mission is to empower aspiring entrepreneurs to acquire and operate businesses through the Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA) model. They provide the tools, capital, and support infrastructure that enables searchers to find, evaluate, acquire, and grow small-to-medium businesses.

## How is Caprae changing the ETA space and broader PE?
Caprae is democratizing access to business acquisition by combining technology-driven lead generation (SaaSquatch) with operational support and capital. Traditional PE requires institutional relationships and large fund structures. Caprae makes search and acquisition accessible to individual entrepreneurs through better tooling and a community-driven approach.
